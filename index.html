About the firm

Engineering, ownership, partnership

SK Design & Engineering LLC was founded in 2026 by Simon Kudernatsch, PhD, a Czech-American engineer and inventor. The firm operates at the intersection of original product design and intellectual property development. Every project is driven by patentable innovation because we believe it solves a real problem, brings genuine joy to its users, and fills a unique niche in the marketplace. We welcome partnerships with established companies to further develop and commercialize our technologies, offer our intellectual property for licensing, patent acquisition, and are open to discussions regarding investment opportunities.
