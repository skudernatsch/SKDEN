Why StrollSki

Say goodbye to staying in. Hello to going out.

01 — Float

Real skis. Real edges.

Full-size downhill, all-mountain, or backcountry skis with hardened metal edges — not short plastic sleds. StrollSki floats through deep, unpacked, and ungroomed snow where existing products stall out.

02 — Fit

Fits any stroller

Three or four wheels, any wheelbase, any width. A sliding rear track, telescoping crossbar, and exchangeable standoffs adjust independently to fit substantially any commercially available stroller.

03 — Safety

Brake & tether built in

A step-on foot brake digs into the snow to hold the stroller in place on any grade, and a waist-strap tether keeps the StrollSki with you at all times on the trail.

Under the hood

The StrollSki in detail

Fig. 1 — Stroller ski system

14

V-groove wheel cradles. Rear and front attachments seat stroller wheels of any diameter and width, secured by a ratchet-strap retention system.

22

Sliding rear track. The rear attachment locks at discrete intervals along a ski-mounted track — matching any stroller wheelbase with one lever.

40

Telescoping crossbar. Sets ski spacing and front-wheel width; twin tongue-and-groove joints keep it rigid, a thumb-screw lock holds it fast.

64

Swap-height standoffs. Exchangeable standoffs lift the front wheels clear of the snow — pick the clearance your trails demand.

76

Foot brake + tether. A push-latch pedal brake bites into the snow on each ski; a waist-strap tether keeps you connected on every descent.
